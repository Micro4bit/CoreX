# Pre-registered spatial validation protocol (draft; no model fitting authorized)

1. **Target:** a positive means a published surface thermal manifestation meeting a documented evidence tier. It does not mean reservoir, economic resource or drilling success. Unobserved locations remain unlabeled/background, never true negatives by default.
2. **Study area:** lock an explicit boundary before sampling. Do not mix partial northern predictors with nationwide background.
3. **Groups:** obtain domain review of independent hydrothermal systems. Until then, use the 18 current units only as provisional co-withholding groups and publish sensitivity to alternative groupings.
4. **Spatial separation:** hold out complete groups and geographic blocks; buffer background samples away from positive coordinates using a preregistered, scientifically justified distance. No random point-wise split.
5. **Leakage:** remove all features derived from the label registry, including distance/density to known manifestations. Fit scalers, encoders, imputation and sampling only on each training fold.
6. **Background:** define reproducible sampling frame and inclusion probability. Evaluate positive–unlabeled assumptions; do not call background negatives.
7. **Comparisons:** begin with a geology-only baseline and regularized logistic regression. Add a justified tree model only under identical folds. Evaluate satellite variables only through a preregistered ablation.
8. **Reporting:** report fold-level uncertainty, calibration limitations, spatial coverage and failure cases. Do not select folds or metrics for attractive performance.
9. **External test:** reserve a geographically separate region/source that is not used for feature selection or threshold tuning.

Authorization requires label/group review, usable permitted predictors across the locked study area, sufficient effective groups, and an approved protocol version.
