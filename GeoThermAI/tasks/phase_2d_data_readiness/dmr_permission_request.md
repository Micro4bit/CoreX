# Draft request to DMR for written permission

Subject: Clarification of reuse conditions for DMR geological-unit and active-fault GIS data

GeoThermAI is a non-predictive research evidence workspace. We request written clarification for the northern 1:250,000 geological-unit service and the “กลุ่มรอยเลื่อนมีพลัง” active-fault dataset (data.go.th package `2f32b01e-6ae7-4b66-aa2c-730a87051793`).

Please confirm separately whether the following are allowed, with required attribution wording:

1. local non-commercial research inspection and spatial intersection/distance calculations;
2. display of transformed/simplified geometry in a browser research application;
3. publication of screenshots containing the layer;
4. export of derived measurements and/or transformed geometry;
5. redistribution of cached GeoJSON or vector tiles;
6. use as an input variable in an academic model, without legal or cadastral reliance.

The catalog labels the license as Creative Commons Attribution but also states a non-commercial restriction and other conditions. Please identify the controlling terms, version, permitted audience and whether derivatives may be shared. Until clarified, GeoThermAI will keep active-fault display, export and distance analysis disabled.
