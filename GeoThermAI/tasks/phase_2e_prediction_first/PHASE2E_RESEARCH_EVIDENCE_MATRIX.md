**Scientific Objective:** Predicting and ranking relative geothermal exploration favorability using multiple geological and geospatial datasets in Thailand.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Literature Framework

GeoThermAI departs from speculative "black-box AI" and adheres strictly to peer-reviewed geothermal prospectivity frameworks established by the U.S. Geological Survey (USGS), international Play Fairway Analysis (PFA) consortia, and contemporary spatial machine learning research.

A primary finding across contemporary literature (Mordensky et al., 2023, 2025; Aliyu et al., 2026) is that **model complexity without data readiness or spatial validation produces catastrophic overfitting, target leakage, and scientifically indefensible exploration claims**. In under-sampled environments like Thailand, establishing rigorous Positive-Unlabeled (PU) sampling (Caraccioli et al., 2023), spatial-group holdout (Buffered Block/Group CV), and clear uncertainty suppression is exponentially more valuable than deploying deep neural networks.

## 3. Methodological Decision Log

1. **Model Hierarchy Locked:** We implement Model A (PFA baseline), Model B (L1/L2 Regularized Logistic Regression), and Model C (Shallow Tree Ensemble). Deep neural networks (e.g. R09) are deferred until data volume and resolution expand.
2. **Positive-Unlabeled (PU) Protocol Formally Adopted:** In accordance with R05 (Caraccioli et al., 2023), non-manifestation cells are treated strictly as unobserved background, never true negatives.
3. **Spatial Buffer & Group Holdout Enforced:** Following R01 and R13, train and test sets are split by independent geothermal system clusters with an exclusion buffer of 20 km to eliminate spatial autocorrelation leakage.
4. **Target Leakage Prohibition:** Features derived from the label registry (such as distance or density to known hot springs) are banned with automated assertion errors in `prediction_architecture.py`.
5. **No Heat-Flow Interpolation:** In accordance with R10, sparse IHFC measurements (154 points in Thailand) are kept as point observations only; continuous 2D surface interpolation is forbidden.
6. **No Speculative Remote Sensing:** In accordance with R06 and Phase 2D findings, remote sensing layers remain disabled until real, QA-validated Landsat surface temperature rasters are acquired.
7. **Uncertainty & Data Suppression:** In accordance with R02 and R11, prospectivity outputs must display an accompanying data coverage uncertainty map that suppresses favorability in unobserved or poorly constrained areas.

## 4. Missing Research Evidence Required Before Predictive ML GO

1. **Independent System Grouping:** Written sign-off from a qualified Thai structural geologist confirming which hot spring manifestations share common hydrothermal recharge/reservoirs versus distinct systems.
2. **Authoritative Label Verification:** Formal resolution of coordinates and licenses for CR_02, CM_16, and the 31 southern provisional records.
3. **Contiguous Predictor Coverage:** Resolution of DMR active-fault reuse permissions and acquisition of continuous, overlapping spatial layers across a locked pilot study area.
4. **External Geographic Validation Area:** Approval of a secondary, geographically distinct test region for out-of-domain evaluation.
