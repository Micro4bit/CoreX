## Key distinction from the 2025 USGS model
The 2025 model uses well-based ordinal signals related to convective heat flow and approximately an order of magnitude more training sites relative to its prior approach. GeoThermAI's current 47 manifestations are not the same data type, cannot support copying USGS labels wholesale, and are not confirmed independent examples. A valid adaptation must document this mismatch explicitly.

## No additional source claims
These sources do **not** establish rights for DMR training, Thai model predictive accuracy, or the existence of full-coverage Thai geothermal measurements. Those claims must await primary evidence.
