## 4. Input Feature Families (Mordensky et al., 2023 Section 2.1)

The original 2023 study utilizes exactly **5 input feature families**:

```
Feature 1: Heat Flow (mW/m²)
  - Description: Terrestrial heat flow compilation derived from conductive gradient measurements and regional crustal modeling.
  - Source: Williams et al. (2008) / Blackwell et al. (2007) SMU Western US heat flow grid.
  - Unit: mW/m² (milliwatts per square meter).

Feature 2: Distance to the Nearest Quaternary Fault (km)
  - Description: Euclidean distance from each grid cell center to the nearest mapped Quaternary (<2.6 Ma) fault trace.
  - Source: USGS Quaternary Fault and Fold Database of the United States.
  - Unit: Kilometers (km).

Feature 3: Distance to the Nearest Quaternary Magma Body (km)
  - Description: Euclidean distance to mapped Quaternary volcanic vents, young volcanic rock contacts, or shallow silicic intrusions.
  - Source: Navdat / USGS volcanic compilation.
  - Unit: Kilometers (km).

Feature 4: Seismic Event Density (events/km²)
  - Description: Spatial concentration of historical earthquake hypocenters reflecting ongoing brittle crustal deformation and fracture permeability.
  - Source: USGS Advanced National Seismic System (ANSS) comprehensive catalog.
  - Unit: Events per km² (or log-transformed density).

Feature 5: Maximum Horizontal Stress (SHmax)
  - Description: Regional tectonic stress magnitude and azimuth determining fault dilation and slip tendencies.
  - Source: World Stress Map (WSM) and USGS stress field modeling.
  - Unit: Megapascals (MPa) / stress ratio.
```
